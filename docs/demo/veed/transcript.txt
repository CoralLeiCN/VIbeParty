Your friends have wild ideas. Vibe Party turns them into a movie night.

Three games bring everyone into a shared video experience: building a story together, competing for votes, and seeing how an idea changes as it passes between people.

Pick a game on your laptop. Friends join from their phones using a room code, on the same Wi-Fi. Then let the fun begin.

First up: Word by Word.

Everyone contributes a secret piece of a shared story: a place, a character, an action, or a consequence.

Imagine a moonlit forest. Add a fox in a tiny hat. Suddenly, it starts breakdancing. Then confetti rains from the sky.

The host reveals each addition in order. You discover everyone's contributions, and watch the scene grow into something only your group could have imagined.

Next is Prompt Royale.

Everyone gets the same topic and writes their own scene. The clips appear together in an anonymous arena, and the group votes for a favourite.

Once voting ends, the authors and results are revealed. Who had the funniest idea? Who took the topic somewhere completely unexpected?

Different imaginations tackle the same challenge, then the room chooses its winner.

Finally, Reverse Prompt: a game of video telephone.

One player writes a scene. The next watches its video and describes it. That description becomes the next video, passed to another player.

At the end, guess the original idea and reveal the whole chain.

The current demo includes labelled sample rounds for trying these flows.

Three games. One shared screen. A room full of ideas.

That's Vibe Party. Pick a game, and roll with it.
